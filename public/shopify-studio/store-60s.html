Consign with us

Bring in what your kids have outgrown. We price it, sell it in the shop and online, and you earn 50%, paid in cash or store credit.

1

Drop off

Any day, no appointment. Clean, seasonal clothing, gear and toys.

2

We price it

Every item gets a tag, a price and a listing, in the shop and online.

3

It sells

Pre-loved items show up right next to the new ones, with a "Pre-loved" badge.

4

You earn 50%

Check your balance any time and get paid in cash or store credit.

Your consignor account

See every item, sale and payout

Consignors log in to see what's in the shop, what sold and what they're owed. The store runs it all from Shopify: items, tags, splits and payouts.
