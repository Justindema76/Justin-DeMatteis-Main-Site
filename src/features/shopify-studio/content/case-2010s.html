What we built

Features that help people buy

Shop by age

Gifts by age, in two taps

Coloured circles for 0–2, 3–5, 6–8, 9–12 and 13+ take parents straight to toys for their child's age. They fill themselves from Shopify's own age data.

Why it matters: it answers the #1 question parents ask in the shop, online.
Jill's picks

The owner's voice on every product

A "Jill's pick" switch and a "Why Jill picked it" note on the product. Picks jump to the front of the homepage rows and get a badge.

Why it matters: customers trust her advice in the shop. Now it sells online too.
Sale badges

Deals customers can actually see

Shopify discounts don't show on the website by themselves. A simple tag adds a "35% Off" badge, and it can switch itself off on an end date.

Why it matters: sales get noticed on the product, not only at checkout.
Shop the shelves

Browse it like walking the aisles

Picture cards for each part of the store, plus product rows with tabs (baby, plush, feeding, essentials), so nobody has to leave the homepage to look around.

Why it matters: less hunting, more adding to cart.

Beyond the theme

Shopify can't do that? We built it.

The rules that free themes and Shopify's built-in settings couldn't handle.

Cart

"Good to know before you check out"

A box at the top of the cart with every store rule: one discount per order, shipping, pickup, returns, final sale and cancellations. Fewer surprises, emails and returns.

Checkout

Free shipping with exclusions

Free Canada shipping over $150, set up automatically, with certain brands excluded. Free Toronto delivery over $150 too.

Discounts

Codes that follow the rules

Promo codes can't be combined with each other or with free shipping, enforced at checkout instead of checked by hand.

Everywhere

One answer, wherever they look

The same rules appear on the product page, in the cart and in the FAQ, so customers always get one clear answer.

Phones

Built for phones, not shrunk

Rows slide on their own and pause on touch, banner arrows sit on the photo, and a back-to-top arrow appears as you scroll.

Catalog

Thousands of products, cleaned up

7,788 products and 145 collections sorted with scripts and backups, so filters and collections stay tidy.

Easy to run

The owner runs it, no code

Every feature came with a simple guide. Staff update everything right in Shopify.

1

Pick favourites

  1. Open a product
  2. Tick "Jill's pick"
  3. Write why, in her own words
Goal: 3–5 picks per department.
2

Give every toy an age

  1. Set the product's category
  2. Choose the recommended ages
  3. Save
The age circles fill themselves.
3

Tag a sale

  1. Add a tag like "35% Off"
  2. Optionally add an end date
  3. The badge appears, then hides
No developer needed.
