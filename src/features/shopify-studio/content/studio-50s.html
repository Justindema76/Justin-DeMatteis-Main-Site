Questions?

Before you ask

How much does it cost?

Most projects are a fixed price based on how many products and pages you have. You get an exact number after your free review, before you commit.

Do I need a premium theme?

Not necessarily. I can take the theme you have well past what it does now, and I'll tell you if a different one would save you money.

Will my store go down while you work?

No. I build on a copy, you review a private preview, and it goes live only when you're happy.

Can my staff still update things?

Yes. Everything is editable in Shopify's theme editor with no code, and you get a how-to guide.
